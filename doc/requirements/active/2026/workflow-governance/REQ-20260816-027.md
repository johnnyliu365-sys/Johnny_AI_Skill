## Required behavior

1. The exception is allowlisted only for this repository and the exact R03-01, R03-02 and
   R03-03 ticket revisions selected by the Senior. It is never a reusable target-project
   fallback and never proves live Router capability.
2. The architecture owner defines the contract only. The Senior remains the sole orchestrator
   and owns exact owner/task/worktree/branch/baseline/model/Context binding and dispatch.
3. R03-01 has no `TicketReceipt`. Its owner-approved `BootstrapDispatchGrant` is an explicit
   temporary authority, not a receipt or `WorkReceipt` union member.
4. R03-02 and R03-03 require a real active `TicketReceipt`. Their bootstrap artifact authorizes
   only the still-missing transport effect and cannot bypass receipt issuance, identity or
   ticket admission.
5. Senior commits an immutable attempt leaf before each host call. That commit consumes the
   grant. `DELIVERED`, `NO_EFFECT` and `EFFECT_UNCERTAIN` are separate additive result leaves.
   A crash, timeout or missing exact result is uncertain and cannot be retried.
6. Every review correction requires a new one-shot correction/transport grant and user relay.
   R03-02/R03-03 corrections retain the same valid receipt when normal same-ticket identity
   remains unchanged.
7. The user relay contains only `BOOTSTRAP_RETURN_AVAILABLE` and `grant_ref`. It wakes the
   Senior but is neither authority nor completion evidence. Senior reads exact committed
   branch/handoff evidence before review.
8. An approved review may automatically produce a separate exact integration grant under this
   already-approved bootstrap policy. Correction, stale main, mismatch or failed validation
   cannot integrate.
9. R03-03 requires separate ticket-specific `HIGH_ASSURANCE` approval. Only reviewed integration
   plus real `CAPABILITY_PROVEN` host/supervision readback changes the project to
   `NORMAL_ACTIVE` and permanently closes the exception.
10. Under `NORMAL_ACTIVE`, Router may automatically dispatch only one uniquely eligible,
    dependency-complete, already-approved low/standard ticket with exact receipt, owner,
    workspace, baseline and supervision proof. High assurance, external effects, new/changed
    requirements, ambiguity or competing candidates still wait for the owner.
11. No heartbeat, automation, cron, watchdog, active-turn wait, recurring thread/Git/filesystem
    read or timed polling is introduced. Before normal activation the user is the only wake
    relay.

## Impact and transition

- Receipt-bound Role Supervision Revision 04 and the Router governance reference own the finite
  bootstrap route. Existing normal dispatch invariants remain unchanged outside the exact
  allowlist.
- `DEC-20260816-521` and R03-01 through R03-03 remain immutable ticketing evidence until the
  Senior creates additive correction/admission leaves under the approved revision.
- Architecture approval creates no grant, attempt, receipt, branch, task, dispatch, review or
  integration. The architecture owner does not choose or bind an Implementer.
- The bootstrap tree remains historical provenance after closure but carries no replayable
  authority. Plugin removal and successor freedom are unchanged.
