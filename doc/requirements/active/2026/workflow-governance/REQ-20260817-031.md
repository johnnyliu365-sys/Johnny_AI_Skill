## Required behavior

1. Bootstrap registry, grant and attempt contracts bind an opaque `target_host_id` obtained only
   from authoritative target-task list/readback. The calling environment, current task host,
   prompt text and inferred/default host are forbidden substitutes.
2. A read-only host admission must prove the exact target task/host pair and host-manager
   resolution before claim-before-effect. Admission failure creates no claim and consumes no
   grant or dispatch operation.
3. Host failures are classified by the effect boundary. Failure to resolve an
   `AppServerManager` occurs before adapter invocation and is `PROVED_NO_EFFECT`; ambiguity only
   at or after invocation remains `EFFECT_UNCERTAIN`.
4. Preserve BDG-003, BDA-003, BDR-003 and settlement commit
   `0b397a08050e435d82632070c2a952c54dcb6d0d` as immutable historical evidence. Add a correction
   decision leaf; do not rewrite or delete those artifacts.
5. The exact BDR-003 incident is proved no-effect by the resolver rejection, target thread
   readback at host `local`, absence of a new target turn and a successful read-only resolution
   from the same Senior context when `hostId=local` was supplied.
6. After an exact Senior correction ticket and independent approval, the already-claimed
   operation may receive exactly one settlement continuation with
   `dispatch_ref=bpb-r03-00-cs02-initial-20260817-003`,
   `claim_commit=ef2a5c1efd5029d8bb5698c0f9c44b0704d1f3d3`, target task
   `019ffb0c-db88-7303-895c-aecfadde7c8d` and `target_host_id=local`.
7. The continuation is not a new grant, attempt or dispatch identity. No BDG-004, BDA-004 or new
   owner grant is permitted for this recovery. A second host delivery call, any identity change
   or any non-proved result halts and routes to Architecture/change control.
8. The efficient execution shape is one read-only target-host admission, one identifiers-only
   host delivery and one bounded post-call readback. Heartbeat, automation, cron, watchdog and
   recurring polling remain forbidden.
