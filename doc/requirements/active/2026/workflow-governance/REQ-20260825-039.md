## Change record

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Old rule | PAI-06 live provider/repository qualification and PAI-07 governance publication were sequential predecessors of PAI-08. Both are external-effect work and therefore remained blocked without a later scope-bound authority, preventing an honest review of the completed core. |
| New rule | PAI-06 is a future per-project operational qualification: it executes only when a project elects `HIGH_COLLABORATION` and receives exact read/effect authority. PAI-07 is a future shipped-governance verification: it executes only when a source-alignment and release authority names the payload, version, tag, installation target, and CLI readback. Neither is a current source dispatch, nor may either create a global `PROVEN` or released claim. |
| Core closure rule | PAI-08 may independently close PAI-01 through PAI-05 after it verifies their committed evidence and verifies that PAI-06 and PAI-07 are explicit deferred verification contracts. Its only success state is `CORE_CLUSTER_CLOSED_WITH_DEFERRED_OPERATIONAL_VALIDATION`; it does not claim live provider enforcement, remote qualification, a published plugin, or CLI installation verification. |
| Effect boundary | This change authorizes requirement, Context, ADR, SPEC and ticket reclassification only. It authorizes no provider command, remote read, push, policy change, release, tag, publication, installation, credential, runner, queue, receipt, descriptor, or host effect. |

## Non-negotiable properties

1. A missing live provider/repository qualification stays `UNPROVEN` or `UNSUPPORTED`; the
   core-cluster closure may never upgrade it to `PROVEN`.
2. A missing governance publication or CLI verification stays unreleased; the core-cluster
   closure may never call the installed payload current.
3. PAI-06 and PAI-07 retain their future evidence requirements and exact owner-effect admission;
   reclassification does not waive, simulate, or replace those controls.
4. PAI-08 is a read-only independent review of committed source and evidence. It may report a
   finite deferred state or a finding, but may not integrate, push, release, configure a
   provider, or perform a live read.
5. The declared-authority-line, direct-readback, non-force, PR/CI non-authority, provider
   enforcement, and three-state bridge properties of `REQ-20260824-038` remain unchanged.

## Acceptance source

The active Context and specification must bind this change. PAI-06 and PAI-07 must each carry a
future-verification purpose, finite honest outcomes, explicit non-effect boundary, and exact
conditions for later admission. PAI-08 must name the reviewed PAI-01 through PAI-05 evidence,
the two deferred-ticket records, and the single non-release success state above.
