| Control-plane disposition 2026-11-19 (generic lesson L-23, under the owner decision "B") | Norms and checks are two mechanisms on two triggers: norms (principles for the whole session) are injected at wake (session start, resume, after compaction); checks (item-by-item on the one thing about to be sent) fire before or after the action, as hooks. Checks come in three strengths chosen by whether a program can decide them: block before the action when decidable and serious (for example a push to a protected branch, a force push, a commit identity other than the repository one); warn first, then block, when decidable but error-prone (for example a new ticket file without its index row); compare automatically after the action (for example a ticket state row against the index). A check reuses the committed gate script for its rule, is fast, depends only on visible input, is tested offline with matching and non-matching input and then once on a real action, and never replaces independent review. Wave 4, beside the context-budget item (L-13 to L-15). The owner may override this disposition. |
