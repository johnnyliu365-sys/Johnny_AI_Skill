## Required behavior

1. `TicketReceipt` is the canonical live execution authority. The older
   `TicketDispatchReceipt` shape is only a pure Router-confirmation projection and cannot be a
   durable authority by itself.
2. One project/ticket may have at most one active or quarantined `TicketReceipt`. Receipts do
   not expire. Same-ticket correction keeps the active receipt unless a bound identity changes;
   replacement requires exact Router revocation first.
3. Approved ticket/handoff records are immutable, exact-commit/digest-bound and durable in
   Johnny-owned metadata. Duplicate identical registration is idempotent; an identity collision
   with different bytes fails before receipt issuance.
4. Before any Agent-control effect, exact readback validates Senior identity, pending descriptor,
   receipt, task, worktree, branch preparation mode, baseline, model/profile, Context epoch and
   complete supervision-chain capability.
5. A dispatch effect is protected by a one-shot claim. `DELIVERED` settles only the dispatch
   claim and leaves the ticket receipt active. `NO_EFFECT` may release the same operation for an
   idempotent retry. `EFFECT_UNCERTAIN` quarantines both operation and receipt and permits no
   automatic retry.
6. The first dispatch envelope remains identifiers only: `ACTION_REQUIRED`, `dispatch_ref`,
   `registry_commit`, `ticket`, `receipt` and `owner_task`. The host adapter resolves and validates
   the exact committed contract; prompts or copied ticket bodies are not authority.
7. Durable live state is metadata-only below the installer-owned Johnny root and is removed with
   the plugin. Target projects retain only their own committed artifacts and opaque historical
   receipt references.
8. This change introduces no heartbeat, recurring read, polling, timer loop, task event
   subscription, network/MCP service, database, new role, task, worktree, dispatch, push, release
   or deployment authority. Missing host or supervision capability remains a typed halt.

## Impact and transition

- Receipt-bound Role Supervision Revision 03 owns the new dispatch admission and settlement
  contracts. Revision 01/02 evidence and the existing Senior admission-decision leaf remain
  immutable.
- The current in-process registry and private Router fake remain deterministic test seams. They
  cannot be presented as a live adapter after this change.
- Exact approval authorizes Senior decomposition of the prerequisite adapter only. Every source
  change still requires a separately admitted implementation ticket and receipt.
