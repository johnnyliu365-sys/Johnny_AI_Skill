## Effective product rule

RWW6 remains non-negotiable: a writer that ignores the advisory lock must not be overwritten by
the managed-artifact runtime. `AtomicConditionalReplace` is an explicit capability, qualified for
one exact operating-system, filesystem backend and filesystem-abstraction combination. A
qualification may be `YES`, `NO` or `CONDITIONAL` only.

`YES` requires a native primitive whose final replace or unlink is atomically conditional on the
target continuing to have the previously observed identity. A separate digest check followed by
`os.replace`, `rename` or `unlink` is not such a primitive. `CONDITIONAL` requires named, runtime-
detectable backend constraints and a proof that every unmatched environment is refused. `NO` is
the correct result when no primitive is proved. An unqualified backend fails closed before the
R09B2 target-document write path and may not claim that advisory locking repairs this absence.

## Non-negotiable properties

1. The capability proof covers the final mutation, not merely a preflight digest comparison.
2. The race model includes a writer that does not take `exclusive-file-lock` and acts after the
   final observed-identity read.
3. Capability evidence is platform/backend-specific. Documentation, API names, unit mocks and
   a different filesystem are not proof for an untested tuple.
4. `CAP-RWW6-01` is an evidence-only investigation. It cannot silently make a runtime write path
   available or turn `f99d836` into a correction candidate.
